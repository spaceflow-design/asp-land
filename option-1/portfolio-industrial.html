01 — ASP Industrial

Guided by a long-term vision, developed as one integrated system.

ASP Land's industrial projects are developed as part of an integrated ecosystem, targeting high-value FDI and advanced industries across Vietnam's key northern growth hubs. Anchored in a comprehensive value chain and a commitment to sustainable development, each project contributes to enabling resilient growth and long-term value for its tenants, workforce, and surrounding communities.

Every park pairs institutional-grade infrastructure with adjacent worker housing and urban amenities — a single-source approach that eliminates the frictions multinationals typically encounter when landing new manufacturing capacity in Vietnam.
