01 — About ASP Land

Building on a strong foundation within the ASP ecosystem.

With roots in shipping, logistics, and infrastructure since 2012, ASP Land is positioned as a developer of industrial and integrated real estate ecosystems. We leverage deep expertise and established capabilities across the value chain to deliver projects that anchor long-term, sustainable growth.

Our work is concentrated in Vietnam's most strategic northern growth corridors — Quang Ninh, Thai Nguyen, Hung Yen, Bac Ninh — where industry, logistics, and urban life converge.
