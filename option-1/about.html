About ASP Land

Beyond real estate, we build enduring foundations.

A next-generation developer of integrated industrial ecosystems — purpose-built to attract high-value FDI and drive Vietnam's growth into a new era.

01 — Introduction

Positioned to shape the next chapter of Vietnamese industry.

Building on more than a decade of operating expertise across shipping, logistics, and infrastructure within the ASP ecosystem, ASP Land is positioned as a next-generation developer of industrial and integrated real estate ecosystems.

Through strategically planned, large-scale developments across Vietnam's most competitive growth corridors, we deliver institutional-grade sites, integrated urban ecosystems, and long-horizon infrastructure — the enduring foundations that anchor multinational capital and generational prosperity.

02 — Our Story

A decade of foundational growth.

2012

Foundation

Entry into international shipping and offshore logistics infrastructure. Fleet scale exceeding 3,000,000 DWT establishes the operating backbone of the ASP ecosystem.

2015–2018

Market Leadership

Became the leading liquid cargo shipping operator in Vietnam. Accumulated the strong financial capacity that underwrites all subsequent development.

2019–2022

Strategic Diversification

Strategic M&A and development of ports and logistics infrastructure in Northern Vietnam. Investment in specialised chemical port terminals and thermal power capacity.

2024

Infrastructure Expansion

Launch of Quang Ninh mega infrastructure projects with total investment exceeding 40,000 billion VND — establishing the ecosystem's northern anchor.

2025

ASP Land Launches

Establishment of ASP Land as a dedicated next-generation developer of integrated industrial and urban ecosystems, purpose-built to attract high-value FDI.

2026–2028

Strategic Coordinate Capture

Complete portfolio activation across Thai Nguyen, Quang Ninh, Hung Yen, and Bac Ninh — the four strategic FDI destinations shaping Vietnam's semiconductor and electronics corridors.

Toward 2035

Integrated Mega-Ecosystems

Long-term vision: pioneer Integrated Mega-Ecosystems combining industrial, urban, logistics, and port infrastructure — all operating under green industrial standards with LEED / Green Building certification.

03 — Vision & Mission

To pioneer Vietnam's next-generation FDI destination.

Vision. Become a leading next-generation FDI destination in Vietnam. Pioneer Integrated Mega-Ecosystems combining industrial parks, urban housing, logistics networks, and port infrastructure — all developed to green industrial standards with LEED-oriented certification and long-term sustainability at the core.

Mission. Develop projects in strategic industrial locations across Vietnam's northern growth corridors. Serve multinational FDI investors with institutional-grade sites, comprehensive value-chain integration, and the operational reliability that global capital demands.
