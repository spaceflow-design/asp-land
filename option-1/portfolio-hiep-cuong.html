02 — Key benefits

Why Hiep Cuong, and why now.

01

Strategic location

Direct expressway access to Hanoi, Hai Phong port, and Noi Bai airport — the tightest logistics triangle in Northern Vietnam.

02

Target industry

Multimodal logistics operators, e-commerce fulfillment, food processing, and last-mile light manufacturing.

03

Integrated model

On-site cross-dock facilities, cold-chain warehouses, and integrated worker housing planned within a 15-minute walk.

04

ESG & sustainability

Rooftop solar mandate for all warehouses. Rainwater harvesting and greywater reuse across the site.

05

Infrastructure advantages

Dedicated truck-access loop road, 24/7 customs support, and bonded warehouse zones for export operations.

03 — Master plan

Phased delivery through 2028.
