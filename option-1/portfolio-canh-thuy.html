Why Canh Thuy, and why now.

01

Strategic location

Positioned at the epicenter of Vietnam's semiconductor cluster — 15 km from Samsung, Amkor, and Hana Micron facilities.

Semiconductor packaging, chip testing, precision electronics, and next-generation display components.

Purpose-built cleanroom pads (ISO Class 5 to Class 8), advanced dry-utility supply, and integrated R&D + housing zones.
