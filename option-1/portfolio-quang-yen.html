02 — Key benefits

Why Quang Yen, and why now.

01

Strategic location

Direct access to Lach Huyen deep-water port and the Hanoi–Hai Phong expressway — the shortest route from Northern Vietnam to global shipping lanes.

02

Target industry

Master-planned for semiconductor packaging, EV assembly, renewable energy equipment, and data-center infrastructure.

03

Integrated model

Adjacent worker housing, technical training campus, and central utilities — a self-contained ecosystem within park boundaries.

04

ESG & sustainability

LEED-oriented masterplan. Circular water and energy systems, on-site solar generation, and preserved green corridors.

05

Infrastructure advantages

Dedicated 220 kV substation, high-capacity fiber ring, private wastewater treatment plant, and 24/7 industrial security.
